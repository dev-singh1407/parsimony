## Note on commit history

The commit history was produced during pair-programming sessions between Dev
Singh and Claude (Anthropic), which is why commits carry a `Co-Authored-By:
Claude` trailer. Commit authorship reflects who ran the session, **not** the
division of intellectual contribution — the design, the corpus, the research
questions and the analysis are the team's shared work.
